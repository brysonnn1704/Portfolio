About Bryson & My Journey

I am a Computer Engineering student pursuing my B.Tech alongside a specialized diploma in Cloud Computing and Big Data in Mumbai, India.

Unlike conventional developers who view design as an afterthought, I believe code and aesthetics belong in constant dialogue. My interest in UI/UX design, visual hierarchy, and graphic design informs how I write front-end code — obsessing over micro-interactions, responsive fluidity, and accessibility.

At the same time, my engineering background in networking, cloud architecture, and cybersecurity gives me the technical depth to build platforms that don't just look stunning, but handle real-world scale and edge cases securely.
